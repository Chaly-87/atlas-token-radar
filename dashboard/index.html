How the number is made. Implied value = TVL × the multiple the market pays per dollar of TVL in that sector × a seven-day growth factor × a maturity factor for time run without a token × a breadth factor for the chains it covers. Launch odds come from the same figures, so a large protocol that may never launch and a smaller one already positioning for a launch are both visible.
